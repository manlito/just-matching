just-matching demo

FlexGlue in this page on your GPU, through WebGPU. Each image is described once; the covisibility levels then pick the pairs worth matching (level 1: the images' mean feature vectors, level 2: a coarse grid through the first matching head), and FlexGlue matches those pairs, stopping early on pairs it judges to show different places (level 3). Keypoints come from just-keypoints (RaCo, best 2048 per image). Nothing is uploaded.
