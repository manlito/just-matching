just-matching in the browser

Runs the WebAssembly module on WebGPU (FP32, and FP16 when the adapter has shader-f16) on the 6 test pairs at 512 px / 9 layers and 384 px / 5 layers, and compares each result with the native CPU backend on the same pixels and keypoints (testdata/). FP32 must give the same matches, predicted points of confident queries within 0.01 px and covisibility within 1e-4; in FP16 at most 3 matches, or 10 % of them, may differ (present on one side only).
